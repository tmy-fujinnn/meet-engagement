How the score works

Each student's score (0 to 100) combines three signals: how many minutes they chatted, spoke or raised a hand (40%; 25% of minutes counts as full marks), how often their camera was on (30%), and how often their Meet tab was in focus (30%). Students who don't run the extension are only observed by others, so tab focus is unknown and the weights become 60% participation and 40% camera. Treat the score as a prompt to check in, not a grade. A camera that's off can mean low bandwidth or a private setting, and quiet students may still be following along.
